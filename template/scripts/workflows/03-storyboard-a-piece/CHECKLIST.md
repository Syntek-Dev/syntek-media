# CHECKLIST.md — storyboard a piece, from approved script to approved boards

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%OWNER_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

> **See** `scripts/docs/reference/storyboards-and-shot-lists.md` and the `storyboard` skill with
> its mode file. This procedure passes gate M3 (scripted → storyboarded). Gates are cited from
> `scripts/docs/reference/the-piece-ladder.md` by number and the move they guard, and never
> restated here.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Read the `storyboard` skill and its mode file. · _sonnet_
- [ ] **The brief is at `scripted` with M2 dated, the script is approved, and the piece has pictures and is not a recording.** · _opus_

## Execution Checklist

**Before boarding**

- [ ] Checked for an existing storyboard and shot list; if they exist, the author confirmed a revision and used IDs are kept. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Script, brief, guides, deliverable presets, brand components, footage manifest, assets and rights register read. · _opus_

**The boards**

- [ ] Storyboard written with frontmatter and one row per board, `B01` onwards. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Every spoken line of the approved script sits in exactly one board's range. · _opus_
- [ ] Every vertical deliverable has its framing on every board; unconfirmed safe zones checked by eye and said so. · _opus_

**The shots**

- [ ] One shot list row per shot, `S01` onwards, each with a source; `to shoot` used where nothing exists yet. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Every `generated` shot agrees with the brief's `ai_visuals`, or the brief's disclosure plan was settled with the author first. · _opus_
- [ ] A `needed` rights row opened or reused for every licensed or identifiable item, its ID in the shot and in the brief's `rights:`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] No voice or likeness boarded without the person's recorded consent, or a row saying it must be obtained. · _opus_

**Approval**

- [ ] Boards and shots read back to the author and corrections applied. · _opus_
- [ ] On the author's approval, `approved:` dated and M3 (scripted → storyboarded) recorded: `status: storyboarded` and `M3` dated in `verified`. · _sonnet_

## Done When

- [ ] **The author has approved the storyboard, and an edit decision list could be written from it without guessing.** · _opus_
- [ ] Nothing was shot, rendered or cleared in this procedure. · _sonnet_
- [ ] The hand-back names the next procedures by their full folder names and offers to start one. · _opus_
