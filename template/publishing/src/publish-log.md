The record of every media deliverable of <%BRAND_NAME%> that has actually gone out: where, when, at which address, and with which disclosure and captions.
Nothing in this repository posts; every row here records what the author reported, in the author's words.
A piece is published when every schedule row for it is `posted` or `dropped` and every `posted` row has its row here.

## Writing rules

1. **One row per upload the author reports**, never for one that is only scheduled or drafted.
   A placement on the brand's own sites, blogs or newsletters is written `<platform>:<slug>` in Platform, its URL the page or issue it went into; a podcast episode served from the brand's own feed has its episode page as its URL.
2. **The URL is the one the author gives.**
   Never guess it, build it from a handle or copy it from another row.
3. **Disclosure set is what the author actually set:** the platform's AI label on or off, and whether the description line went in.
   Where it differs from the post package, say so in Notes and report it.
4. **Captions** records the caption file uploaded, `burned`, or `none` with the reason.
5. **Dates are DD/MM/YYYY.**
6. **Never delete or rewrite a row.**
   A mistake is corrected by a dated note under `## Corrections`, so the history of what went out stays whole.
