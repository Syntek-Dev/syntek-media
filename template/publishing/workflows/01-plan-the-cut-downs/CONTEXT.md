# CONTEXT.md — publishing/workflows/01-plan-the-cut-downs/

The procedure for turning one finished master into an agreed plan of short cuts: which moments
stand alone, for which platforms, with which lines, In and Out, frame and hook. It reads the
brief, the script or transcript, the master's timing and the guide for each platform; it proposes,
and the author decides. It renders nothing (the cuts are made by
`publishing/workflows/02-cut-for-a-platform/`), and it moves no piece up its ladder.

## When to use this

- A piece's master is finished (its brief dates M4) and the piece is to go out as short cuts as
  well as, or instead of, its full-length deliverables.
- An existing plan needs another cut, or a cut needs re-timing or reframing before it is rendered.
- The author asks 'what can we cut from this?'

Reach for a **different** procedure when the task is rendering an agreed plan, or the piece's
full-length deliverables alone (`publishing/workflows/02-cut-for-a-platform/`), or a social media
plan or content calendar (the social-media-documents skill (syntek-author), where present).

## What it produces, and where

- **A cut-down plan** at `publishing/src/cut-downs/<piece>.md`, every row approved by the author.
- **A hand-back** naming each cut, its deliverables, every `verify` key it relies on, which cuts
  need their own thumbnail, and the next procedure.

## The failure this procedure exists to prevent

A batch of cuts rendered on instinct: moments that lean on what came before, hooks buried behind
a warm-up, a speaker cropped out of a vertical frame, and a run of near-identical clips that
YouTube's inauthentic-content rule treats as mass-produced. Each costs a render and a review, and
once posted, the account's reach. Agreeing the plan first, line by line and frame by frame,
prevents all of them.
