# FICTION.md — media-teach, fiction mode

The families a novelist learns media in, and the house sources each practises against. A book's
media is made from the book, so every practice piece invents its own story, people and places
instead of the book's.

## Paths and unit

- **Workspace:** the topic's folder (`SKILL.md`, 'Where a topic lives'), and a temporary folder
  outside the repository for any clip or render.
- **Unit:** one lesson. Its practice piece imitates one piece (a trailer beat, a reading's script,
  a board row, an edit decision list or a post package) but never sits in `scripts/src/pieces/`,
  is never registered and never takes a number.
- **House sources:** the guides in `scripts/docs/reference/`, `production/docs/reference/`,
  `publishing/docs/reference/` and `brand/docs/reference/`; the toolkit's own help;
  `brand/src/voice/voice.md`, read as reference and never edited; the book's manuscript and
  style sheet, where syntek-author's layers are present (under manuscript/src/ and standards/),
  read for how the real work does it and never quoted into a practice piece.

## Additions to the steps

- **Step 2 — also record the family:** craft, tool, platform or book media.
- **Step 4 — also,** a book media lesson's primary source is the rule itself (a retailer's or a
  platform's own specification, a music library's licence, the terms a publisher or agent sets on
  promoting the book), read through `media-research` and dated, never recalled.
- **Step 5 — also,** a practice piece uses an invented story with invented people in an invented
  place: never the book's cast, plot or invented names, so nothing practised is mistaken for the
  book, and never a name syntek-author's names register already holds, where present.

## Domain rules

| Family | What is practised | House source |
|---|---|---|
| Craft | a hook that sells the premise without the ending; writing a reading for the ear; a board that shows mood without a cast; pacing a cut to music | `scripts/docs/reference/writing-for-the-ear.md`; `scripts/docs/reference/storyboards-and-shot-lists.md`; `production/docs/reference/edit-decision-lists.md` |
| Tool | the toolkit's presets, cuts and checks; `card.py` and the card layouts; captions and loudness; Claude Design for a cover-led look | `toolkit/CONTEXT.md`; `publishing/docs/reference/captions.md`; `production/docs/reference/sound-and-loudness.md`; `brand/docs/reference/claude-design.md` |
| Platform | reading the platform data; a cut-down that stands alone; a post package; the AI label | `publishing/docs/reference/platform-specs.md`; `publishing/docs/reference/cut-downs.md`; `publishing/docs/reference/posting-and-the-log.md`; `publishing/docs/reference/ai-disclosure.md` |
| Book media | spoilers and what a trailer withholds; a character's voice described for a narrator; quoting the book aloud and showing its cover; a reading's credits; where the project makes audiobooks, the narration guide | `production/docs/reference/character-voices.md`; `production/docs/reference/rights-and-consent.md`; the audiobook narration guide, where the project makes audiobooks, and the trailers guide, where it makes trailers |

- **Invented everything.** A lesson never reuses the book's people, places, languages or plot,
  because a practice trailer that turns out well tempts a shortcut into the real one.
- **A character's voice is described, never generated, in a lesson.** Designing a real character
  voice spends credits, and runs only through its own procedure, on the author's word.

## Examples

- **Mission (craft):** 'Cut a trailer that makes people want the book without telling them the
  twist.' First lesson: three invented hooks for a story about a lighthouse keeper, sorted by what
  each gives away.
- **Mission (tool):** 'Lay out a quote card myself.' First lesson: a card layout for an invented
  line, rendered with `card.py` into a temporary folder outside the repository, then checked at
  thumbnail size.
