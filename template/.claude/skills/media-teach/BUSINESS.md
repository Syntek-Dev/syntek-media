# BUSINESS.md — media-teach, business mode

The families a business owner learns media in, and the house sources each practises against. A
business's real pieces carry its clients, customers, staff and figures, so every practice piece
invents all four.

## Paths and unit

- **Workspace:** the topic's folder (`SKILL.md`, 'Where a topic lives'), and a temporary folder
  outside the repository for any clip or render.
- **Unit:** one lesson. Its practice piece imitates one piece (a brief, a script beat, a board
  row, an edit decision list, a cut-down plan or a post package) but never sits in
  `scripts/src/pieces/`, is never registered and never takes a number.
- **House sources:** the guides in `scripts/docs/reference/`, `production/docs/reference/`,
  `publishing/docs/reference/` and `brand/docs/reference/`; the toolkit's own help; the brand's
  `brand/src/voice/voice.md` and `brand/src/design-system/tokens.css`, read as reference and never
  edited; syntek-author's brand voice and brand guide, where present, read the same way.

## Additions to the steps

- **Step 2 — also record the family:** craft, tool, platform or business media.
- **Step 4 — also,** a business media lesson's primary source is the rule itself (a platform's
  own branded-content or advertising policy, the regulator's guidance on paid promotion, a music or
  stock library's commercial licence, a release form's terms), read through `media-research` and
  dated, never a summary of it; note the region the rule holds for.
- **Step 5 — also,** every practice piece uses invented businesses, clients, staff and figures
  (for example Harbour Lane Studio and its customer Robin Example). **Never a real client, a real
  customer's face or name, or the business's real prices or results.**

## Domain rules

| Family | What is practised | House source |
|---|---|---|
| Craft | a hook in the first seconds; writing for the ear; a call to action a viewer can act on; a storyboard with a picture for every line; an edit that holds one idea per shot | `scripts/docs/reference/writing-for-the-ear.md`; `scripts/docs/reference/storyboards-and-shot-lists.md`; `production/docs/reference/edit-decision-lists.md` |
| Tool | the toolkit's presets, cuts and checks; `card.py` and the card layouts; captions and loudness; Claude Design for the brand kit | `toolkit/CONTEXT.md`; `publishing/docs/reference/captions.md`; `production/docs/reference/sound-and-loudness.md`; `brand/docs/reference/claude-design.md` |
| Platform | reading the platform data; a cut-down that stands alone; a post package; the AI label | `publishing/docs/reference/platform-specs.md`; `publishing/docs/reference/cut-downs.md`; `publishing/docs/reference/posting-and-the-log.md`; `publishing/docs/reference/ai-disclosure.md` |
| Business media | consent and releases for staff and customers on camera; paid partnerships and their disclosure; licensed music and stock for commercial use; claims about the business said aloud | `production/docs/reference/rights-and-consent.md`; `publishing/docs/reference/ai-disclosure.md`; the rule itself, through `media-research` |

- **A practice package is never paste-ready.** It carries no real handle, link, price or
  discount code, so nothing from a lesson can be posted by mistake.
- **Never practise on a live client job.** Invent the client; a practice piece that names a real
  one will one day be read as real.
- **A claim practised is still a claim.** A lesson that writes a result or a figure marks it
  `VERIFY` or invents it plainly, and says so.

## Examples

- **Mission (craft):** 'Open a product explainer so people stay past the first three seconds.'
  First lesson: three invented hooks for Harbour Lane Studio's booking service, timed by ear and
  compared.
- **Mission (business media):** 'Know when a post needs a paid-partnership label.' First lesson:
  the platform's own policy read through `media-research`, then three invented posts sorted by
  whether they need one, each with its reason.
