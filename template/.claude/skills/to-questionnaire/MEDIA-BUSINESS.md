# MEDIA-BUSINESS.md — to-questionnaire, media business mode

Who a business's media work asks, and what never becomes a questionnaire: a licence, a release
and a consent are records in their own right, and a questionnaire may only gather what fills one.

## Paths and unit

- **Unit:** one blocked decision about a piece, the brand kit or a platform, and the one person
  outside the session who can unblock it.
- **Questionnaires folder:** the path `.claude/rules/syntek-media/02-skills.md` Section 4 gives
  that role; where none is agreed yet, agree one with the author before step 2.
- **Voice guide:** `brand/src/voice/voice.md` for the spoken voice, beside syntek-author's
  written voice, where present, for the questionnaire's own words.
- **The block:** under the `Open questions` heading of `.claude/MEMORY.md`, and in the `## Notes`
  of the brief whose decision waits on it.

## Additions to the steps

- **Step 1 — also test the media boundaries below first.** Then settle what the recipient may
  see: never an unpublished script, cut, take or price unless the author says so.
- **Step 2 — also ask for what a media decision needs**: a date, a duration, a deliverable, a
  wording the recipient must approve, or whether they will appear on camera.
- **Step 3 — also strip every media path and tool name**: the recipient sees a piece's working
  title and what it is for, never a folder, a register ID or a flag.
- **Step 5 — also** route an answer that grants a right to its row in
  `production/src/rights-register.md` through `production/workflows/07-clear-the-rights/`.

## Domain rules

| Recipient | Register |
|---|---|
| A client or colleague who will appear or be named | the house voice, plain; say where the piece will be posted |
| A venue, a designer, an editor or a voice artist | plain and specific; dates, durations and deliverables first |

- **A licence, a release or a consent, a voice clone's included:**
  `production/workflows/07-clear-the-rights/`, under
  `.claude/rules/syntek-media/03-production-ethics.md` Section 6; a questionnaire gathers only the
  details it needs.
- **A social media document's questions:** the social-media-documents skill (syntek-author), where
  present (`.claude/rules/syntek-media/02-skills.md` Section 4).

## Examples

```text
# When can we film the workshop?

**Purpose:** fix the day and what may be shown before the explainer is scheduled.
**From:** Harbour Lane Studio · **To:** the workshop manager · **By:** 31/10/2026

## The day
### Which morning is quietest?
1. Tuesday — two benches in use
2. Thursday — one bench in use

**Recommended:** Thursday, so no one is filmed who has not agreed to it.

> (your answer)
```
