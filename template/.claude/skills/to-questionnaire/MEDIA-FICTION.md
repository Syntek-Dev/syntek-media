# MEDIA-FICTION.md — to-questionnaire, media fiction mode

Who a novelist's media work asks, and what never becomes a questionnaire: a licence, a release
and a consent are records in their own right, and a questionnaire may only gather what fills one.

## Paths and unit

- **Unit:** one blocked decision about a piece, the brand kit, a character's voice or a
  platform, and the one person outside the session who can unblock it.
- **Questionnaires folder:** the path `.claude/rules/syntek-media/02-skills.md` Section 4 gives
  that role; where none is agreed yet, agree one with the author before step 2.
- **Voice guide:** `brand/src/voice/voice.md` for the spoken voice and every character's.
- **The block:** under the `Open questions` heading of `.claude/MEMORY.md`, and in the `## Notes`
  of the brief whose decision waits on it.

## Additions to the steps

- **Step 1 — also test the media boundaries below first.** Then settle what the recipient may
  see: never an unpublished chapter, an unreleased cover or a line past the piece's reveal line
  unless the author says so.
- **Step 2 — also ask for what a media decision needs**: a date, a cover or art file and its
  terms of use, a narrator's availability, or how a name is said in the author's own reading.
- **Step 3 — also strip every media path and tool name**: the recipient sees the book's title
  and the piece's purpose, never a folder, a register ID or a flag.
- **Step 5 — also** route an answer that grants a right (a cover, an illustration, a quotation
  from a review) to its row in `production/src/rights-register.md` through
  `production/workflows/07-clear-the-rights/`.

## Domain rules

| Recipient | Register |
|---|---|
| A cover designer, an illustrator or a publisher's rights contact | precise; the use, the platforms and the dates first |
| A narrator or a voice actor | plain; the character, the tone and the length of the work |

- **A licence, a release or a consent, a voice clone's included:**
  `production/workflows/07-clear-the-rights/`, under
  `.claude/rules/syntek-media/03-production-ethics.md` Section 6.
- **Approaching a reader for a blurb or an endorsement:** the author, or syntek-author's work,
  where present (`.claude/rules/syntek-media/03-production-ethics.md` Sections 1 and 7).

## Examples

```text
# May the cover appear in a moving trailer?

**Purpose:** clear the cover for the launch trailer before it is boarded.
**From:** Morgan Example · **To:** the cover designer · **By:** 31/10/2026

## The cover
### May the cover's layers be moved apart on screen?
1. Yes — the lighthouse can rise from the sea in the opening shot
2. No — the cover is shown whole and still

**Recommended:** yes, if your licence allows animation; say if it does not.

> (your answer)
```
