# MEDIA-NONFICTION.md — to-questionnaire, media non-fiction mode

Who a non-fiction author's media work asks, and what never becomes a questionnaire: a licence, a
release and a consent are records in their own right, and a questionnaire may only gather what
fills one.

## Paths and unit

- **Unit:** one blocked decision about a piece, the brand kit or a platform, and the one person
  outside the session who can unblock it: a guest, a co-host, a venue, or a translation's
  publisher.
- **Questionnaires folder:** the path `.claude/rules/syntek-media/02-skills.md` Section 4 gives
  that role; where none is agreed yet, agree one with the author before step 2.
- **Voice guide:** `brand/src/voice/voice.md` for the spoken voice, beside syntek-author's
  written voice, where present.
- **The block:** under the `Open questions` heading of `.claude/MEMORY.md`, and in the `## Notes`
  of the brief whose decision waits on it.

## Additions to the steps

- **Step 1 — also test the media boundaries below first.** Then settle what the recipient may
  see: never an unpublished chapter or an unedited recording unless the author says so.
- **Step 2 — also ask for what a media decision needs**: a guest's topic and the points they will
  not discuss, a venue's filming terms, or a translation's terms for audio and for video.
- **Step 3 — also strip every media path and tool name**: the recipient sees the episode's or
  talk's title and its purpose, never a folder, a register ID or a flag.
- **Step 5 — also** route an answer that grants a right to its row in
  `production/src/rights-register.md` through `production/workflows/07-clear-the-rights/`.

## Domain rules

| Recipient | Register |
|---|---|
| A podcast guest or a co-host | plain and warm; the episode's question, its length and how it will be published |
| A translation's publisher or a venue | precise; the use (audio, video or both), the platforms and the dates |

- **A licence, a release or a consent, a guest's release included:**
  `production/workflows/07-clear-the-rights/`, under
  `.claude/rules/syntek-media/03-production-ethics.md` Section 6.
- **A claim an expert supports:** the fact-check skill (syntek-author), where present, or a named
  source (`.claude/rules/syntek-media/03-production-ethics.md` Sections 1 and 7).
- **Whether a passage needs a sensitivity reader:** syntek-author's work, where present.

## Examples

```text
# What should the episode cover?

**Purpose:** agree the conversation before the recording is booked.
**From:** Robin Example · **To:** the guest · **By:** 31/10/2026

## The conversation
### Which part of your work should we start from?
1. The open-table project — the episode's question in practice
2. Your book — wider, and it needs a second episode

**Recommended:** the open-table project, so the episode answers one question well.

> (your answer)
```
