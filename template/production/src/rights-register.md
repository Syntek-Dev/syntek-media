The register of everything a piece uses that someone else owns, or that shows or sounds like a real person, and the state of its clearance.
`storyboard` opens a `needed` row for every licensed or identifiable item it plans, and the author requests and records each permission.
A piece is scheduled only when every row it uses is `cleared`.
`production/docs/reference/rights-and-consent.md` explains each kind and each status.

## How to add a row

- The ID is the next in sequence from `RR0001`, permanent and never reused.
- The Kind column takes one of `music · stock · footage-release · location · likeness · voice-consent · quotation · scripture · font · artwork · generated`.
- The Status column takes one of `needed · requested · cleared · refused · expired`.
- The Pieces column lists the piece folders that use the item, separated by commas; a new use is added to the same row.
- The Evidence column says where the licence, release or consent is filed; a document that holds personal data stays outside the repository.
- The Expires column is DD/MM/YYYY, or `—` when the permission does not expire.
- Never record a permission as cleared without the permission in hand.
- Never delete a row: a refused or expired item keeps its row, with the date and the reason in Notes.
