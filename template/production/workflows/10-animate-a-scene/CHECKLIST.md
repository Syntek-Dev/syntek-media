## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read the brief, script, re-timed boards, shot list and accepted timing files. · _opus_
- [ ] Type `scene` confirmed; preceding sub-checks current, dated or reasoned `n/a`. · _opus_
- [ ] Brand art and tokens supplied; approved sound named in the edit. · _opus_

## Execution Checklist

- [ ] Real sources indexed, findings resolved and accepted through `-o`, then committed. · _sonnet_
- [ ] One scene defines native layouts and the author-set frame count/rate. · _opus_
- [ ] Typed text, walk poses, facing, blinks, breathing and anchor interactions timed. · _opus_
- [ ] Edit has audio only; joined voice, music/effects, fades, ducking and cue links checked. · _opus_
- [ ] First/middle stills made for every board at every required native aspect. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Every printed still opened; mouths and picture checked with the author. · _opus_
- [ ] Box findings resolved; Timing notes routed through the timing procedure. · _opus_
- [ ] Current preview master played and agreed by the author after any notes. · _opus_
- [ ] Every further aspect rendered natively, frame counts/sound/loudness verified. · _sonnet_

## Done When

- [ ] `M4.stills` dated after preceding sub-checks; M4 recorded only when all hold. · _opus_
- [ ] `ai_visuals: assisted`, source versions and `last_updated` current. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Tracked sources committed; no page, report or render hand-edited or committed. · _sonnet_
- [ ] All master paths handed back, with approval evidence and outstanding rights. · _opus_
