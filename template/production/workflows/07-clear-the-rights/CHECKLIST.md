# CHECKLIST.md — clear the rights

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%OWNER_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

> **See** `production/docs/reference/rights-and-consent.md`. This procedure serves the rights part
> of M7 (captioned → scheduled); gates are cited from `scripts/docs/reference/the-piece-ladder.md`
> by number and never restated here.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] **The piece is confirmed, and its brief, shot list or transcript, edit decision list and script are found.** · _opus_

## Execution Checklist

**Gathering**

- [ ] Every item listed: brief `rights`, shot-list Rights, footage `rights`, music and effects, a cloned voice's consent, quotations, scripture, fonts, artwork, generated material. · _opus_
- [ ] Each item matched to its row, the piece added to Pieces; a missing row opened as `needed` with the next ID. · _sonnet_

**Checking**

- [ ] Each `cleared` row's Evidence names where the permission is filed. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Each permission covers this use: platforms, territory, duration and medium. · _opus_
- [ ] No row expires before the scheduled date. · _sonnet_

**Requesting and recording**

- [ ] Each missing permission requested by the author, never by Claude; rows set to `requested` with the date. · _opus_
- [ ] Each answer recorded with its evidence and expiry; nothing marked `cleared` without the permission in hand. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Each refused, expired or unanswered item cut, replaced or re-shot with the author, through its own procedure. · _opus_

## Done When

- [ ] **Every item the piece uses has a `cleared` row naming the piece, or has been cut or replaced.** · _opus_
- [ ] No row was deleted, and every refusal keeps its date and reason. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Handed back: every row and its status, requests outstanding, items cut or replaced, whether the rights part of M7 is met. · _opus_
