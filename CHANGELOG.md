## [Unreleased]

### Fixed

- **The audits are steady on a CI runner** (`.github/scripts/` only; nothing a project receives changes). Every Git command an audit starts, Copier's included, now runs with `gc.auto=0` and `maintenance.auto=false` (`sm_git_quiet` in `_common.sh`), because a background `git gc --auto` or `git maintenance` left running after a commit wrote into a scratch repository while it was being removed: `update-test.sh` stopped on a GitHub runner with "rm: cannot remove '…/proj/.git': Directory not empty". Per-kind cleanup in `update-test.sh` and `coexist-test.sh` retries once (`sm_rmtree`).
- **A failed toolkit self-test names its failing cases.** `toolkit-smoke.sh` check 1 now carries the self-test's `FAIL` lines (up to three) before its last line, so a failure that happens only on a runner, whose scratch folder is gone when the job ends, can be read in the CI log.

### Added

- **Three platforms for the brand's own channels** (D53, D61): `website` (the sites the brand publishes media to, its own and any it writes for: self-hosted or embedded video, posters and share images), `blog` (posts on those sites: video, transcripts, featured and share images) and `newsletter` (email issues: a linked preview image or GIF, never video). One `website` platform covers every site; a site is a row of the profile's `## Sites`, named by a frozen slug, and a list a row of the newsletter profile's `## Lists`. Each adds its profile seed, `brand/src/platforms/<platform>.md`, empty of rows with its `AUTHOR TO CONFIRM` flag, and its guide, `publishing/docs/reference/<platform>.md`. No brand kind defaults to any of them (D54).
- **Placements** (D61): one deliverable going to one page of a profile site or one issue of a list, the one file that page or issue plays or shows first, written `<platform>:<slug>` in the schedule and the publish log and as its post-package section's qualifier, with only the words media owns (D55): an embed's or figure's title, alt text, Links to, the structured-data values and the agreement. A site or list the brand does not own needs its profile row's dated agreement, recorded once per site, before a placement there is `ready`. An embed of the YouTube upload is a placement of `youtube.long`, never a deliverable of its own.
- **A self-hosted podcast** (D58, D59): one RSS feed per show, written by `media.py feed` from the show register `publishing/src/podcast/<show>.toml` and served, with the episode audio, art, captions and chapters, from one site the brand owns; Apple Podcasts and Spotify ingest it, and YouTube carries each episode as video uploaded into the show's podcast playlist. New: the pair-only folder `publishing/src/podcast/` (gated `'podcast' in PLATFORMS`, its skeleton fenced in its `CLAUDE.md`), `publishing/workflows/08-publish-the-podcast-feed/` (a feed episode's part of M7: the show opened once, the episode's row, `feed tag`, `feed chapters`, `feed check`, the upload copy and the author's upload list) and `publishing/docs/reference/podcast-feed.md` (the feed, the two routes to an episode, the hosted route, the order and time of upload, the hosting tests as `curl` lines, a corrected file, YouTube's two routes, the validators). The tracked `<show>.feed.xml` is written only after the author reports the feed live, by `publishing/workflows/06-record-a-publication/`.
- **Published transcripts** (D60): `publishing/src/captions/<piece>[--cNN].transcript.en-GB.md`, of exactly what one page plays, for every piece or cut with speech placed on a profile site and for every feed episode, made with `captions transcript` and described by hand.
- **New toolkit commands, cited by the content** (D57, D59, D60): `media.py image` (posters at `--at`, and every JPEG, WebP or AVIF of a rendered PNG), `cut --overlay` and the GIF preview, `captions transcript`, and `feed new`, `add`, `tag`, `write`, `chapters` and `check`. Rules 04 Section 2 lists them, and Section 3 names the tracked feed as the one tracked file the toolkit overwrites.
- **New deliverable keys cited by key, never by number** (D26, D57, D58): `website.video`, `website.video_720`, `website.hero_loop`, `website.poster`, `website.poster_720`, `website.og_image`, `blog.video`, `blog.poster`, `blog.featured_image`, `blog.og_image`, `newsletter.preview_image`, `newsletter.preview_gif`, `podcast.feed_audio` and `podcast.id3_cover`, with the platform-wide `[platform.website]`, `[platform.blog]`, `[platform.newsletter]` and `[platform.podcast]` tables.
- **Examples:** `business.answers.yml` turns on `website`, `blog` and `podcast` (a talk published as an episode, so its media kinds are unchanged); `author-fiction.answers.yml` turns on `blog` and `newsletter` (a blog without a website); `author-nonfiction.answers.yml` turns on `website`. Together they still turn on every platform and media kind, and `examples/CONTEXT.md` and `examples/CLAUDE.md` now say so for all nine platforms.
